      FAQ

      What does the AI score mean?

      Detectors provide probabilistic estimates and can flag human writing. The local demo score illustrates the interface and has no calibrated probability.

      What does the similarity score cover?

      It is the share of analyzed words matched in the material actually retrieved or supplied. Search providers may return only snippets or abstracts. Review each match and citation in context.

      What happens to my text?

      Text is processed by this server and held in your current workspace until cleared. External processing is opt-in. External providers apply their own retention policies.
